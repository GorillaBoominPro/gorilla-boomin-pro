ⓘ About App
G//BMN: SIGNAL DJ · Browser-based DJ console prototype. Local audio playback, mixer EQ, effects and basic recording depend on browser support.
Not certified for professional club use. Some hardware-style controls and streaming integrations are still in development.


🎓 CLUB STANDARD TRAINING


START SMALL. PLAY BIG. Train with an affordable controller while learning the signal flow and techniques used in professional DJ booths.


Training profile: Select the hardware you want to practise with. This does not claim automatic controller compatibility.






TRAINING OBJECTIVES


  • Club deck navigation: play, cue, loop, pitch and jog control
  • Four-channel mixer principles: gain, EQ, filters, cue and crossfader
  • Performance workflows: beatmatching, phrasing, transitions and recording
  • Advanced features roadmap: stems, beatgrids, controller layers and smart cueing


Available prototype: Virtual decks, mixer controls, track imports, crates and experimental scratching. Not yet verified: FLX4 plug-and-play mapping, reliable real-time stems, hardware-level latency or professional booth certification.



Educational simulation only. Hardware feel, layout and controls can differ from actual club equipment.
