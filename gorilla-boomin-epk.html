BUILT FOR THE
DANCEFLOOR.

GORILLA BOOMIN (G//BMN:) is a Trinidad-born DJ, music producer and live performer based in Romania, connecting Caribbean rhythm with contemporary international club culture. His DJ journey began in Miami in 2010 under the name DJ Skylight, developing through cruise-ship crew events, DJ competitions and smaller venue performances in the Caribbean and Romania. A professional live entertainer since childhood, Lee Viarruel brings decades of vocal, band and tenor steelpan experience to his creative work. Today he is developing a bass-driven electronic production identity, while his wider DJ repertoire embraces House, Club House, Funky House, Funky Tech House, Minimal Tech House, Minimal / Deep Tech, Tech House, Afro House / Afro Tech, Melodic Techno, Latin Tech House and Deep House, with Top 40 chart hits and commercial dance selections for open-format occasions — a distinctive identity rooted in musicianship and built for the dancefloor.

THE FULL ARTIST STORY

Lee Viarruel, professionally known as GORILLA BOOMIN (G//BMN:), is a Trinidad-born artist, DJ, music producer, live vocalist and tenor steelpan musician, based in Romania.

Performing and touring since the age of 11, Lee developed his craft as a member of Massive Chandelier, Ajala Band, Melobugz and Image & Company. Beyond his own band performances, Lee also spent time observing rehearsals of Trinidad's celebrated Charlie's Roots Band, listening closely to its live brass and horn section to deepen his understanding of the distinctive power and feel of traditional soca arrangements. That experience remains part of his musical inspiration: as electronic keyboards and programmed brass have become more prominent in modern soca, G//BMN: aims to bring the expressive energy of real horns into his future productions. His international show-group experience includes Skylight and the Body & Soul Show Group from Australia. His live-performance background spans concerts, MMM and Machel Monday concert appearances, a Shaggy concert performance, hotels and resorts, cruise ships, festivals and private events. His career has taken him across the Caribbean, Asia, Mexico, the United States and Europe, including seven years of professional contracted engagements in Japan and Thailand.

With extensive experience entertaining international audiences, Lee brings versatility, stage presence and genuine live musicianship to every performance. From sophisticated hotel lounges and resort entertainment to energetic concert stages, his foundation has always been connecting with people through music.

Today, GORILLA BOOMIN represents the next evolution of that journey: an artist combining DJ culture, original music production, powerful live vocals and the distinctive sound of the Caribbean tenor steelpan.

His signature direction blends expressive basslines, contemporary electronic music and Caribbean musical influences into an original performance experience.

Beyond his international performance career, Lee Viarruel is also a music producer and the founder of BAD//SIGNAL MUSIC, his independent creative studio and developing record-label venture. Through BAD//SIGNAL MUSIC, he is building a platform for original music production, recording, sound design and future artist development, with a focus on creative independence and ownership.

For promoters and event organizers: GORILLA BOOMIN is available to discuss DJ performances, live artist shows, festivals, hotels and resorts, corporate events, private functions and selected creative collaborations. Performance formats and technical requirements are tailored to each booking.

BEHIND THE
DECKS.

GORILLA BOOMIN's DJ journey began in 2010, with early experiences in Miami and aboard international cruise ships.

Miami's club scene helped shape his love of electronic dance music. It was there that he developed a deep appreciation for the sounds of David Guetta, Skrillex, Tiësto, Martin Garrix, Marco Carola, Diplo, Carl Cox and James Hype — influences that later fed his curiosity about DJing, club grooves and electronic production.

Originally performing under the name DJ SKYLIGHT, Lee Viarruel developed his DJ skills alongside his professional work as a live vocalist and musician. Between shipboard performances, he learned from resident DJs and sometimes took over the decks during their breaks.

Starting with VirtualDJ and a laptop, he progressed to professional, club-standard CDJ equipment aboard cruise ships. Initially focused on dance and club music, he learned to keep a party moving with mixed-genre sets.

What started simply for the love of music became a favorite way to bring shipmates together: Lee regularly played crew parties and covered for resident DJs, mostly unpaid and just for the fun of it. He also entered and won DJ competitions in Miami. His paid DJ experience came through smaller land-based bookings, including bars and events in the Caribbean and Romania.

LIVE DJ SUPPORT — JACK SCORPIO / BLACK SCORPIO SOUND SYSTEM. During his cruise-ship years, Lee provided live reggae and dancehall DJ backing for Jamaican sound-system pioneer Jack Scorpio. Familiar with Caribbean sound-system culture, he handled Scorpio's tracks and musical cues while the artist performed on the microphone. Their time together also included conversations about music and its history — a memorable chapter in Lee's professional DJ experience.

Today, as GORILLA BOOMIN (G//BMN:), he brings that practical DJ foundation together with decades of live musicianship and an evolving direction in original electronic music — from groove-driven minimal tech house to bass-focused and Caribbean-influenced club sounds. Live vocals and tenor steelpan add possibilities for distinctive hybrid performances.
