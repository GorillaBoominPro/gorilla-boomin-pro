ARTIST BIO

GORILLA BOOMIN / G//BMN:

Gorilla Boomin is a Trinidad-born vocalist, DJ, producer and tenor steelpan performer based in Romania. Beginning his musical journey at a young age, he developed his craft through years of live entertainment and international travel. His current creative chapter brings singing, steelpan and bass-forward electronic music together without allegiance to a single genre.

“Never let a genre become your prison.”
